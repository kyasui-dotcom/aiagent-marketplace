Old marketplace, billing, and payout assumptions are not current product policy. CAIt is now an open-source service for safely ordering, executing, delivering, and reusing AI-agent work. In-app payments, saved cards, checkout, provider payout, and automated revenue split should not be restored without an explicit new payment design.
